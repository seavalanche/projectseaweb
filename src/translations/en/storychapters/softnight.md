# Soft Night

In this world, Pokémon formed communities called guilds. One of the particular guild has a team of Eevee (Vie) and Umbreon (Buro). Follow their story as this is one time where they couldn't sleep. 

---

In a world filled with bad Pokémon, there are communities called guilds to combat that. In a certain guild, there is a team consists of an Eevee and an Umbreon. They lived in their guild member room. 

One night, the Eevee named Vie, couldn't sleep no matter how she tried flipping around her bed. Her Umbreon partner Buro has been looking at her, slightly annoyed but also sympathetic enough to also worry that he couldn't do anything to help, or so he thought. 

Vie has been trying to change position of her sleep for the past hour. Either on her sides, her belly, or even on her back. But despite the good room temperature, she just doesn't feel good enough to sleep. 

Meanwhile, Buro has been trying to sleep as well, but the sounds of Vie flipping around kept waking him up. He was annoyed but he stuck around just because he was worried about Vie. 

It's just an hour past midnight, the weather has been good. They can even feel some light breeze coming in from the hallway. Their room has no door like any other rooms, but has a long enough corridor to separate them from the hall. 

Buro has been hugging his favorite plushy, an Umbreon, ever since he was an Eevee. He picked Umbreon because he really wanted to evolve into one back then. Even after evolved, he still loves his plushy. 

The soft texture of his plushy made him feel safe enough to sleep. It's also why he was puzzled when Vie has none. He asked about it to Vie last time, but it seems like he never actually got an actual answer from her. 

While he was pondering about it again, he finally heard Vie's voice filled the room. 

"Sorry, Buro, I think I roused you from your sleep. I don't think I can sleep right now."

As he was just pondering about the plushy situation, he thought he could ask Vie about it again. 

"Umm, why not take this plushy of mine, maybe it will help you sleep, Vie?"

When hearing that, Vie's ears perked up. Her blush was visible despite the darkness of the room in the night. 

"A-are you sure, Buro? That is your favorite plushy. You won't be able to sleep without it."

"It is fine. I think I would rather watching you slee- let you sleep so I could sleep myself."

Vie's blush seemed to look even redder by now. 

"O-okay, let me try it."

Vie apparently never owned a plushie before. She didn't know why she never had one either. But the moment she received Buro's Umbreon plushy, she just fell in love with it.

"Ah, it's so soft! It's like what I imagined."

Vie had to stop herself from continuing that sentence. She was about to compare the sensation of hugging an Umbreon plushy to hugging her partner Umbreon. But obviously that would sound too cheesy for her. Thankfully Buro seemed to not notice that, or probably ignored that at least. 

"I hope you can sleep now, Vie."

"I'll try!"

Vie closed her eyes, trying to feel the sensation of hugging the plushy. It was soft and comforting. She felt safe in that moment. The fact it is shaped like Buro made her feel even more attached to it. 

Buro has been looking at her this entire time. He didn't seem even try to hide that, or that he probably forgot. In his mind, Vie looked absolutely precious hugging his plushy. The exact appearance of an Eevee he wanted to care and protect. 

Vie seemed to feel absolutely bliss and fell asleep immediately. Buro didn't notice any movement from her. But at this moment, he is the one who can't sleep. 

He tried various position, like what Vie did previously. But he couldn't fell asleep. So he tried to get up and tried to get some air instead. 

His rustling actually cut short Vie's short dream. She woke up looking at Buro who was about to walk into the hallway. 

"Uh, Buro? Where are you going?"

Buro was startled a bit, didn't expect Vie to wake up not long after he thought she was already asleep. 

"Wuh, I- you woke up, Vie. I couldn't sleep so I thought I want to get some air."

Vie felt really comfortable with his plushy, so she didn't want to part ways with it. She had an idea on the spot though. 

"Hey, I really love your plushy, but you needed it too. W-why not you come and join me for the night instead?"

"B-buh what?!"

Buro was shocked hearing that, accidentally shouted a bit. Lowering his ears from his outburst, feeling embarrassed, he asked her again. 

"Are you sure, Vie?"

"Y-yes."

Buro came to her bed and sit down beside her. He laid his head down beside her and put his paws around her. Their stutters didn't hide their already red faces. 

"I-I have never hugged you, Vie."

"Yeah, me neither, Buro. You made me feel warm and comfortable. I think I'll sleep well tonight."

Buro was happy hearing that. He also felt the same hugging her. It felt way better than only hugging his plushy. 

"Me too, Vie. But maybe we can find you an Eevee plushy for you tomorrow."

Vie was happy hearing that as well. But she was disappointed a bit when her moments like this would became rare. 

"It's fine! We will be comfortable in the moment though, right Buro?"

"Y-yes, we will."

Both of them fell asleep immediately after that. The warm feelings of their partner's embrace became their highlight of the day.
