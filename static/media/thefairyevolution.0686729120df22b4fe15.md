# The Fairy Evolution

In a faraway world, there are creatures which we call Pokémon. They are quite different from what we imagine an animal to be. Each Pokémon has various abilities on their own, either to defend themselves, or just playing with it to color their lives.

In this world, there are many bad guys lurking around on every corner, waiting to do malicious things. To combat that, many Pokémon communities decided to form an organization that they called guilds. The guild members are tasked to help the regular citizens from those bad guys.

This story will be focused on a particular Pokémon called Eevee, which are named Vie. She is a female eevee that struggled to pick an evolution. While she was accepted as a guild member together with another eevee called Buro, she is still quite anxious that she couldn't do her job due to her lack of confidence in her own abilities.

One night, she found out that her childhood friend, Buro, has managed to evolve into an Umbreon while they were watching the moon together in their den.

"Wow! Congratulations, Buro! I read the book from the guild's library about the evolutions of Eevee, but it seems like you chose Umbreon!"

Buro replied with a smug, "Hmpf, of course! I wanted to be big and strong! So I can protect y- uh the citizens, of course!"

Vie seems to ignore the stutter, "That's a really good reason, Buro! I wish I know which one to pick. From the book that I read, it seems like I have eight choices. They are all so cool and cute and strong and also cute!"

"Of course! But I will not protect you even if I am on your side because I needed a strong partner."

Vie seemed to be annoyed, "Hey! I will protect you if I am on your side, why don't you want to protect me?"

"Hey, you chose to partner with me to enter the guild. You gotta do what you need to do to proof yourself to be my partner."

"Fineee... Let's just sleep, we got an important mission tomorrow remember? The guildmaster wouldn't be happy if we failed this 'small' mission like what he called it."

Buro unfazed, "Nah, we will be fine!"

The next morning, at the guild hall, after the guild morning briefing.

Vie started, "Okay, we have to seek this bad guy who stole a bag from a child."

"Yeah, and it seems like the bad guy is a Hitmonlee. Why would a Hitmonlee steal from a kid?" Buro confused.

"I... don't know either. But this is our tenth mission as a team, we better not fail it!"

"Yeah, yeah, get this over with."

So the team went to a nearby forest, where a reported sight of the said bad guy was last seen.

After checking her bag, Vie said, "Okay, we should be ready to enter. Let's go!"

Buro weirdly stopped her in her tracks, "Wait, do you bring the reviver seeds?"

"Ah, I forgot! But it's half an hour from the town. My mistake for not checking before coming here."

Buro, while holding one reviver seed, said, "Ah alright, I will prot- uh watch you fight the bad guys from the sidelines and see your butt get kicked!"

Vie was annoyed, "Why do you insist on me getting butt kicked? We are partners, you should help me. Let's just go into the forest already."

After several floors of the mysterious dungeon forest, they arrived at a weirdly unusual clearing.

Vie anxiously said, "Uhh, we ran out of Oran berry. And this place gives me creeps."

"Yeah, obviously. You ate like 10 Oran berry." Buro commented while ignoring the last part.

"Hey! You just watched me on the sidelines doing nothing but letting the weird Pokémons kicked my butt!"

Buro feinting happiness, "Yeah! I like seeing you struggle, of course!"

"Wh-," Vie wanted to reply back but was disrupted by someone.

"Well, well, well, I think I am caught here, am I not?" A sound came out from the sides of the clearing, startling Vie.

The Pokémon appeared, it's the wanted Hitmonlee! Buro subconsciously went in front of Vie.

The startled Vie regained some senses back, "Hey! You! Give back the bag!"

The Hitmonlee smirked, "Heh, I don't really need the bag, I just wanted a challenge. It's hard to find a willing Pokémon to be my spar partner, so I stole a bag to get an attention!"

"What kind of tactic was that? In any case, I'll let you face Vie instead," annoyed Buro said.

Vie shocked, "What? This guy looks strong! And from the typing class we got, I learned that I am weak to fighting moves!"

Buro shrugged, "And? Me too, I am weak to fighting moves. I have seen you got your butt kicked many times today, I doubt this guy can beat you down, Vie."

"Save your chitchat and fight me already. I don't care if you both fight me together, I just wanted some action. Here, the bag you wanted," the Hitmonlee threw the bag to Vie.

Vie caught the bag, "Thanks, but no thanks! I don't want to get my butt kicked again! But hey you also have done a bad thing today, you needed to be brought to the guild to be punished!"

"Then beat me in a battle!"

"Uhh, okay then." Vie then looked at Buro, "Buro please help me."

"Nope, nope! I wished I got popcorn, now please start!" Buro said while moving to the sidelines to watch.

The annoyed Vie ignored his last comment. She then charged towards the Hitmonlee. The battle surprisingly lasted for 10 minutes, but Vie was on the brink of fainting.

Vie panting badly, "I... can't... go... o-"

Before she fell to the ground, Buro swooped in and carried her to the side after watching the fight the entire time. He gave Vie the reviver seed he has been carrying the entire time. Her eyes was already closed due to the fatigue.

"Please work, please work," Buro muttered.

Vie suddenly woke up with a new vigor. Her first sight after opening her eyes was Buro's entire face being no further than a few centimeters away.

"Aaa!" Vie sprung to her feet once more. But then calmed down after realizing what has happened. "Buro? W-why do you decide to help me now?"

"U-uh, my paws slipped, yes of course." Buro tried to be convincing, but it failed.

"Of course you were! You liked me but you aren't going to say it because you thought it is cool, isn't it?"

"N-n-no no no, of course it was not!"

Vie smiled, "Hehe, I love you too, Buro."

She was enveloped in a bright light, even the Hitmonlee who was watching from afar was blinded by it for a moment.

After the light dimmed, Vie appeared to look different. She has underwent an evolution! Her new appearance was shrouded in a mystical fairy aura.

In a new form, Vie shouted, "Kyah! I have evolved into Sylveon! Thanks to you, Buro!"

Buro was visibly blushing, "Buh, m-me?"

Vie smiled, then look at the Hitmonlee who walked closer.

"It seems like I have found a new opponent!" The Hitmonlee said then suddenly charged towards Vie. The battle continued.

After a few seconds, Vie managed to wrap the Hitmonlee with no efforts at all, rendering him unable to move.

"I... lose..." The Hitmonlee realized it way too late.

Buro immediately ran towards Vie, then hugged her, "I am so sorry, Vie. I didn't mean to be a bad partner."

Vie hugged back, "Hehe, I know. You realized that I can't reach an evolution such as Sylveon if I never got maximum affection to someone, right?"

Buro seems to regain the blush back, "B-buh uh yeah." He stayed silent for a bit then continued, "Love you too, Vie."

After the event, they went back to the guild and reported to the guildmaster. The Hitmonlee was punished to clean the city for a week. Both Vie and Buro went back to their den to watch the moon together again.

"Thank you again, Buro." Vie starting to wrap Buro with her ribbons.

"Uh, you are welcome, Vie. Sorry again!" Buro said while embracing her ribbons.

Vie went and hugged Buro, "You are so cute when you blush!"

"Sthappp!" Buro said while he went and hug Vie back.

The End.
